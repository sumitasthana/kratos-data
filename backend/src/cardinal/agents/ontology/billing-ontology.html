The one story worth understanding: the grace period

Follow the amber boxes. This is the rule the law forces in, and the one most people model wrong.

  1. Did they pay last month's bill in full? The app looks back one month (the purple arrows) and answers yes or no.
  2. If yes, the grace period is on. Grace is a no-interest window: new spending is not charged interest.
  3. So interest gets switched off. That is the dashed amber arrow. During grace, interest for the month is zero.
  4. If no, interest is charged as the average balance times the monthly rate.
