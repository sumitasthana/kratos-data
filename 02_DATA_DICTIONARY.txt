ENUM: interest_calc_method_enum
--------------------------------------
Values:     Simple, Compound, Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually
Purpose:    How interest is calculated on account balance
Usage:      ACCOUNT.interest_calculation_method

Details:
  - Simple: I = P × R × T (no compounding)
  - Compound: I = P × (1 + R/n)^n - P (depends on frequency)
  - Daily: Calculated daily
  - Monthly: Calculated/posted monthly
  - Quarterly: Calculated/posted quarterly
  - Semi-Annually: Twice per year
  - Annually: Once per year

SECTION 6: CONTROL MAPPINGS (PART 370 CONTROLS → DATA COLUMNS)
================================================================================

CONTROL A1: ORC Assignment at Account Opening
  Data Elements:
    ACCOUNT_REGULATORY_CLASSIFICATION.orc_code
    ACCOUNT_REGULATORY_CLASSIFICATION.orc_determination_date
    ACCOUNT_REGULATORY_CLASSIFICATION.orc_determination_method
    ACCOUNT.account_open_date
  Validation:
    orc_code NOT NULL
    orc_determination_date = account_open_date (assigned at opening)
    orc_determination_method documented
  Audit Query:
    SELECT account_id, account_number, orc_code, orc_determination_date
    FROM account a
    JOIN account_regulatory_classification arc ON a.account_id = arc.account_id
    WHERE arc.orc_code IS NULL OR arc.orc_determination_date > CURRENT_DATE;

CONTROL A2: ORC Validation Audit
  Data Elements:
    ACCOUNT_REGULATORY_CLASSIFICATION.orc_code
    ACCOUNT_REGULATORY_CLASSIFICATION.orc_verification_date
    ACCOUNT_OWNERSHIP (count of owners if Joint)
    FIDUCIARY_ARRANGEMENT (if Trust)
  Validation:
    orc_verification_date ≤ 12 months old
    If is_joint_ownership=TRUE → ACCOUNT_OWNERSHIP has ≥2 owners
    If is_trust=TRUE → FIDUCIARY_ARRANGEMENT exists
  Audit Query:
    SELECT arc.account_id, arc.orc_code, arc.orc_verification_date,
           DATEDIFF(CURRENT_DATE, arc.orc_verification_date) as days_since_verification,
           COUNT(ao.owner_party_id) as owner_count
    FROM account_regulatory_classification arc
    LEFT JOIN account_ownership ao ON arc.account_id = ao.account_id
    WHERE arc.orc_verification_date < CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '365 days'
       OR (arc.is_joint_ownership AND COUNT(ao.owner_party_id) < 2);

CONTROL A3: Fiduciary Documentation
  Data Elements:
    FIDUCIARY_ARRANGEMENT.document_file_reference
    FIDUCIARY_ARRANGEMENT.document_verification_date
    FIDUCIARY_ARRANGEMENT.latest_amendment_date
  Validation:
    document_file_reference NOT NULL
    document_verification_date ≤ 24 months old
    latest_amendment_date documented if trust modified
  Audit Query:
    SELECT fa.account_id, fa.fiduciary_type, fa.document_verification_date
    FROM fiduciary_arrangement fa
    WHERE fa.document_file_reference IS NULL
       OR fa.document_verification_date < CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '730 days';

CONTROL A4: Fiduciary Beneficiary Data
  Data Elements:
    FIDUCIARY_BENEFICIARY.distribution_percentage
    FIDUCIARY_BENEFICIARY.beneficiary_verification_date
    FIDUCIARY_BENEFICIARY.beneficiary_status
    FIDUCIARY_BENEFICIARY (count per fiduciary)
  Validation:
    SUM(distribution_percentage) per fiduciary ≈ 100%
    beneficiary_verification_date ≤ 24 months old
    beneficiary_status tracks active vs. deceased/removed
  Audit Query:
    SELECT fa.fiduciary_id, SUM(fb.distribution_percentage) as total_percentage,
           COUNT(fb.beneficiary_id) as beneficiary_count,
           MIN(fb.beneficiary_verification_date) as oldest_verification_date
    FROM fiduciary_arrangement fa
    LEFT JOIN fiduciary_beneficiary fb ON fa.fiduciary_id = fb.fiduciary_id
    GROUP BY fa.fiduciary_id
    HAVING SUM(fb.distribution_percentage) NOT BETWEEN 99 AND 101
        OR MIN(fb.beneficiary_verification_date) < CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '730 days';

CONTROL A5: POD/TOD Designation
  Data Elements:
    ACCOUNT_FEATURE.feature_type = 'POD_PayableOnDeath' or 'TOD_TransferOnDeath'
    ACCOUNT_FEATURE.beneficiary_party_id
    ACCOUNT_FEATURE.beneficiary_designation_form
    ACCOUNT_FEATURE.beneficiary_form_on_file
    ACCOUNT_FEATURE.beneficiary_verification_date
  Validation:
    beneficiary_form_on_file = TRUE
    beneficiary_verification_date ≤ 24 months old
    beneficiary_party_id references valid PARTY
  Audit Query:
    SELECT a.account_id, a.account_number, af.feature_type,

    DEPOSIT_INSURANCE_CALCULATION.calculation_test_error_message
  Validation:
    calculated_insured_amount ≤ $250,000 per owner
    calculated_insured_amount + calculated_uninsured_amount = input_account_balance
    Test result = 'Pass' for all test cases
    Formula: 
      Single: min(balance, 250K)
      Joint (2 owners): min(balance, 250K * 2) [e.g., if balance=$400K, insured=$400K, uninsured=$0]
      Trust (3 beneficiaries): min(balance, 250K * 3) [e.g., if balance=$500K, insured=$500K, uninsured=$0]
  Audit Query:
    SELECT dic.calculation_id, dic.account_id,
           dic.input_account_balance, dic.calculated_insured_amount,
           dic.calculated_uninsured_amount,
           dic.input_account_balance - (dic.calculated_insured_amount + dic.calculated_uninsured_amount) as imbalance,
           dic.calculation_test_result, dic.calculation_test_error_message
    FROM deposit_insurance_calculation dic
    WHERE dic.calculation_test_result = 'Fail'
       OR ABS(dic.calculated_insured_amount + dic.calculated_uninsured_amount - dic.input_account_balance) > 0.01
       OR dic.calculated_insured_amount > 250000;

Query 1: ORC Accuracy Audit (Control A1/A2)
------
SELECT
  arc.account_id,
  a.account_number,
  arc.orc_code,
  COUNT(ao.owner_party_id) as active_owner_count,
  CASE
    WHEN arc.is_joint_ownership AND COUNT(ao.owner_party_id) < 2 THEN 'FAIL: Joint but <2 owners'
    WHEN arc.is_trust AND NOT EXISTS(SELECT 1 FROM fiduciary_arrangement fa WHERE fa.account_id = a.account_id) THEN 'FAIL: Trust but no fiduciary record'
    WHEN DATEDIFF(CURRENT_DATE, arc.orc_verification_date) > 365 THEN 'FAIL: ORC verification >12 months old'
    ELSE 'PASS'
  END as validation_result
FROM account a
JOIN account_regulatory_classification arc ON a.account_id = arc.account_id
LEFT JOIN account_ownership ao ON a.account_id = ao.account_id AND ao.ownership_end_date IS NULL
WHERE a.account_status IN ('Active', 'Dormant')
GROUP BY arc.account_id
HAVING validation_result LIKE 'FAIL%';

Query 3: Coverage Calculation Status (Control C2)
------
SELECT
  arc.orc_code,
  COUNT(DISTINCT dic.account_id) as account_count,
  SUM(CASE WHEN dic.calculation_test_result = 'Pass' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as passed,
  SUM(CASE WHEN dic.calculation_test_result = 'Fail' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) as failed,
  SUM(dic.calculated_insured_amount) as total_insured,
  SUM(dic.calculated_uninsured_amount) as total_uninsured,
  CAST(SUM(dic.calculated_insured_amount) / SUM(dic.input_account_balance) * 100 AS DECIMAL(5,2)) as coverage_percentage
FROM account_regulatory_classification arc
JOIN deposit_insurance_calculation dic ON arc.account_id = dic.account_id
WHERE dic.calculation_date = CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '1 day'
GROUP BY arc.orc_code
ORDER BY total_insured DESC;

Query 4: Uninsured Deposit Exposure
------
SELECT
  a.account_id,
  a.account_number,
  a.current_balance,
  dic.calculated_insured_amount,
  dic.calculated_uninsured_amount,
  CAST(dic.calculated_uninsured_amount / a.current_balance * 100 AS DECIMAL(5,2)) as uninsured_percentage

SELECT
  a.account_id,
  a.account_number,
  af.feature_type,
  p.individual_name_family as beneficiary_name,
  af.beneficiary_form_on_file,

Query 6: Fiduciary Account Audit (Control A3/A4)
------
SELECT
  fa.account_id,
  fa.fiduciary_type,
  fa.document_verification_date,
  COUNT(fb.beneficiary_id) as beneficiary_count,
  SUM(fb.distribution_percentage) as distribution_total,
  MIN(fb.beneficiary_verification_date) as oldest_beneficiary_verification,
  CASE
    WHEN fa.document_file_reference IS NULL THEN 'FAIL: No document file'
    WHEN DATEDIFF(CURRENT_DATE, fa.document_verification_date) > 730 THEN 'FAIL: Doc verification >24 months'
    WHEN SUM(fb.distribution_percentage) NOT BETWEEN 99 AND 101 THEN 'FAIL: Distribution % not ~100'
    WHEN MIN(fb.beneficiary_verification_date) < CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '730 days' THEN 'FAIL: Beneficiary verification stale'
    ELSE 'PASS'
  END as validation_result
FROM fiduciary_arrangement fa
LEFT JOIN fiduciary_beneficiary fb ON fa.fiduciary_id = fb.fiduciary_id

APPENDIX B: GLOSSARY
------
SMDIA
  Standard Maximum Deposit Insurance Amount
  Currently $250,000 per depositor per ORC per institution
  Set by Congress; last changed to $250K in 2010

ORC
  Ownership Right & Capacity
  Classification of how account is held (single, joint, trust, IRA, etc.)
  Determines FDIC deposit insurance coverage per Part 330

EOD / End-of-Day
  Close of business day (typically 5:00 PM ET)
  When daily account balances are calculated and reconciled

CIP / Customer Identification Program
  Regulatory requirement per FinCEN (31 U.S.C. § 5318)
  Bank must verify identity of customers before opening accounts

KYC / Know Your Customer
  Broader compliance program; includes CIP plus ongoing monitoring
  Helps prevent money laundering, terrorism financing, etc.

PEP
  Politically Exposed Person
  Person holding/related to public office; higher-risk classification

OFAC
  Office of Foreign Assets Control (US Treasury)
  Maintains sanctions list; banks must screen customers/transactions

GL / General Ledger
  Master accounting records; ultimate source of truth for bank balances

POD / Payable-on-Death
  Account designation; beneficiary receives funds upon account holder's death

TOD / Transfer-on-Death
  Similar to POD; state-law mechanism for designated account transfer

IDI
  Insured Depository Institution
  Bank, savings bank, or credit union that accepts FDIC insurance

Sweep Account
  Automatic transfer of funds between linked accounts
  Typically used to move excess to money market or savings for higher interest

Regulation D
  Fed regulation limiting certain types of account withdrawals
  "6 withdrawals per month" rule for savings accounts (may be waived)

Regulation CC
  Fed regulation defining when deposits must be available
  Requires timely availability of deposited funds
